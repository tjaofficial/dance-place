About The Dance Place

QUALITY TRAINING.
JOY INCLUDED.

We believe dancers deserve both: education that helps them improve and an environment that reminds them why they fell in love with dance in the first place.

Our PhilosophyTeach the dancer.
Build the person.

Individual needs matter. Proper placement matters. Confidence matters. Whether a student dances for fun or dreams of a professional future, we meet them where they are and help them take the next step.

Nearly Five Decades

A STUDIO WITH ROOTS — AND FORWARD MOTION.

The Dance Place has spent nearly 50 years helping dancers become stronger performers, confident students and lifelong fans of dance. Alumni have gone on to careers as professional dancers, choreographers, educators and more.
