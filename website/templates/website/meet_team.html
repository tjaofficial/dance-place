Owner & Director

Chelarrie Goldsby

A Detroit dancer, instructor and choreographer dedicated to inspiring dancers at every age and level.

Read Chelarrie's story +

Chelarrie Goldsby is the proud owner and director of The Dance Place. A respected dancer, instructor and choreographer from Detroit, she has dedicated her career to teaching and inspiring dancers of all ages and levels. She is deeply connected to the Waterford dance community and also serves as a professional adjudicator for local and national competitions.

Her performance background includes featured roles in The Radio City Christmas Spectacular as Clara, Showboat, The Harlem Nutcracker and The Wizard of Oz. She was also a regional champion in the USA Gymnastics Junior Olympics.

Chelarrie studied film and television at the Specs Howard School of Broadcasting Arts and has spent more than two decades building a reputation for captivating, high-energy and athletic choreography. Her performance credits also include work alongside artists such as Kashdoll, Flo Rida and TLC.

At the center of it all is education. Chelarrie is passionate about helping dancers reach their potential while building creativity, discipline and confidence on and off the stage.

Instructor & Choreographer

Stacey Manzanares

More than two decades of teaching, performance and nationally recognized competitive choreography.

Read Stacey's story +

Stacey Manzanares has taught Dance Place students for more than two decades, inspiring recreational dancers to love the art while training serious dancers for professional opportunities. For 20 years she has choreographed for Corps De Danse, The Dance Place's competitive team, with work recognized nationally.

Her professional performance career includes work with the Detroit Symphony Orchestra in The Nutcracker, where she danced principal roles for several years, along with Dance Detroit/Jacob Lascu, Dayton Ballet, Dreams/Anna Sokolov, Michigan Opera Theatre, Circles/Alana Barter, Easter Seals/Ed Asner and Pat Paulson & Max Davies Dancers.

Instructor & Choreographer

Jessica Manzanares

A Dance Place student turned educator whose choreography has earned awards nationwide.

Read Jessica's story +

Jessica Manzanares grew up knowing she wanted to teach at The Dance Place and continue the quality dance education she began receiving at age three. She started her professional teaching and choreography career at 16 and has taught Technique, Ballet, Tap, Jazz, Hip Hop and Lyrical.

Her choreography has earned top awards at dance competitions nationwide, with professional work spanning competitive and recreational dance, industry and video projects, Oakland University's Dance Team and Tinderbox Productions in Detroit.

Jessica also toured with Hall Of Fame Dance Challenge, giving her the opportunity to study talented choreographers from across the United States.

Faculty

Alex Hlavaty

Professional dancer, choreographer and writer with national and international performance experience.

Read Alex's story +

Alex Hlavaty began formal dance training at age six and earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Dance with a minor in Creative Writing from Western Michigan University in 2015. His studies have taken him to New York City with Gibney Dance and Montréal with O Vertigo and Les Ballets Jazz de Montréal.

He joined Eisenhower Dance Detroit and has performed and taught master classes nationally and internationally. Alex joined The Dance Place faculty in 2018 and is also an avid choreographer and writer, with work appearing in festivals, intensives, competitions, Culture Pop in Detroit, the literary journal The Laureate and DancePlug online.

Faculty

Kendal Dockter

Dance Place trained, award-winning and known for classes that build creativity and self-empowerment.

Read Kendal's story +

Kendal Dockter trained at The Dance Place from ages three through 18. She was a principal dancer in the Pre-Pro Dance Team and became an award-winning choreographer.

Kendal began teaching in 2018 and quickly proved herself to be a creative and inspiring educator. Her classes promote self-empowerment, and Dance Place students love her energy.

Faculty

Brianna Purrett

Dance Place alumna with professional training and experience spanning concert, ballroom and commercial dance.

Read Brianna's story +

Brianna Purrett graduated from Western Michigan University with a Bachelor of Arts in Dance before continuing her education in New York City at Broadway Dance Center, studying under Heather Rigg and Deborah Y. Wilson.

While in New York, she worked for Fred Astaire Dance Studio in Westwood, New Jersey as a Ballroom instructor and performer before returning to Michigan to teach Ballroom, Ballet, Stretch, Latin and Jazz.

Her choreography and professional dance credits include Palace Events, News Channels 4 & 7, wedding and bridal shows, Detroit Music Hall and national ballroom events. A Dance Place/Corps De Danse alumna, Brianna teaches Preschool, Ballet, Jazz, Technique, Ballroom and Wedding Dance.

Office Manager

Patience Sawyers

The friendly face of the front desk since 2016 — known around the studio as the “Desk Diva.”

Read Patience's story +

Patience Sawyers has been the friendly face of the front desk since 2016 and currently serves as The Dance Place Office Manager. She is known for getting things done with kindness and efficiency.

Patience is hearing impaired and reads lips, and the studio affectionately knows her as the “Desk Diva.” She is Stacey's sister, Jessica's auntie and one of the people who helps keep the ship sailing.
