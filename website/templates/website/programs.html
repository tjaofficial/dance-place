Explore Our Programs

CHOOSE THE EXPERIENCE THAT FITS YOUR DANCER.

Recreational Dance

Recreational Program

Progressive, age-appropriate training for dancers who want to learn, grow and enjoy performing.

→

Our traditional dance-season pathway builds technique, choreography, confidence and stage experience throughout the school year, with opportunities to perform in professionally organized spring recitals.

Accelerated Dance

Accelerated Program

Focused training for experienced dancers who are teacher-recommended for the accelerated pathway.

→

Accelerated study may include additional technique classes, summer intensives, ballet workshops, guest artists and expanded performance opportunities. Placement is handled by studio staff.

Competition

Performance & Competition Programs

Additional stage opportunities for dancers interested in performance, competition and community events.

→

Selected dancers can participate in performance classes, parades, productions, regional and national competition opportunities and community appearances.

See performance opportunities →
Short-Takes

Six Week Sessions (Short-Takes)

Short-format sessions designed for younger dancers and families who want a flexible introduction to dance.

→

Short-Takes include Mommy & Me and Pre-School offerings in a shorter six-week format that makes it easy for young dancers to get started.

Programs and dates change throughout the year.

Specialty Programs

Private Lessons & Specialty Classes

Flexible specialty training for dancers, adults, couples and families looking for something beyond the traditional class schedule.

→

Offerings may include Ballroom, Summer Dance, Wedding Dances, Private Lessons, workshops and seasonal intensives.

Programs and availability may change throughout the year.
