How Registration Works

Three simple steps.

Start with the basics, find classes that match your dancer, then complete registration through Encore.

01 Learn The Basics

Explore Your Options

You do not need to know everything before you begin. Open any question below for a quick explanation, then move to Step 2 when you're ready to find classes.

What programs are available?+

Choose a program below to see a short description.

What level should I select?+

Levels move from beginner foundations through advanced and professional training. If you are unsure, leave the level open when you search. Accelerated placement is teacher recommended, and the studio can help place your dancer correctly.

1 Beginner / Base Skill 2 Experienced / Beginner 3 Intermediate / Competitive 4 Advanced 5 Professional
What styles are available?+

Styles include Ballet, Tap, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acrobatics, Pointe and Technique, with additional specialty offerings available throughout the year.

What age groups are available?+

The Dance Place offers options from preschool through adult. Here are the age groups:

Mommy & Me — 18 months–3 years Pre-School — 3 years–4 years 5–7 8–11 12–18 Adult — 18+

In Step 2, enter the dancer's exact age so the live schedule can show classes that match more accurately.

How to estimate your tuition

Add your family's total weekly class hours.

Each additional hour uses the next rate in the list. A half-hour is charged at half of the next hourly rate.

Example 3½ weekly hours
$65 + $57 + $52 + $23 = $197/mo
The $23 is half of the 4th-hour rate of $46.

This guide is intended to help families estimate monthly tuition. Final enrollment and payment details are completed through Encore.
