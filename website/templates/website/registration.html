How to estimate your tuition

Add your family's total weekly class hours.

Each additional hour uses the next rate in the list. A half-hour is charged at half of the next hourly rate.

Example 3½ weekly hours
$65 + $57 + $52 + $23 = $197/mo
The $23 is half of the 4th-hour rate of $46.

This guide is intended to help families estimate monthly tuition. Final enrollment and payment details are completed through The Studio Director.
